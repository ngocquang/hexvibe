HEXVIBE Privacy Policy

Cat Studio · Effective date: October 26, 2026

HomeSupportTiếng Việt

Summary

  • You can play HEXVIBE without an account. Then your progress stays on your device, and we collect no personal data.
  • Sign-in and leaderboards are optional. They are for players 16 or older.
  • If you sign in, we keep your account ID, your username, and your scores so that the leaderboards work.
  • We do not sell your data. This version of HEXVIBE has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Cat Studio makes HEXVIBE ("the app"). Cat Studio controls the personal data that this policy describes. To contact us, send an email to minhlong2225@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

The app keeps this data in a file on your device. We do not receive it.

  • Your progress, your best scores, and your settings (for example the language and the sound volume).
  • The result of the age check. The app keeps only "16 or older" or "under 16". It does not keep your birth year.

If you delete the app, this data goes away.

3. Data we collect when you sign in

Sign-in is optional. Only a player who is 16 or older can sign in. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we collect this data:

DataWhy we use it
Account ID from Firebase Authentication, and the email address that Apple or Google gives us. If you use "Hide My Email" with Apple, we get a relay address, not your real address.To identify your account and keep you signed in.
The username that you chooseTo show your name on the leaderboards. Other players can see it.
The result of each run: score, length of the run, number of moves, phase reached, end time, run ID, and app versionTo rank your scores on the leaderboards and to find scores that are not possible in normal play.

We do not get your Apple or Google password. We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, or device advertising ID.

4. What other players can see

The leaderboards show your username and your scores to all players. They do not show your email address.

5. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions) to run sign-in and the leaderboards. Google stores this data for us on servers in Singapore (Google Cloud region asia-southeast1). Google processes the data only to give us this service. For more information, read the Firebase privacy information.

We do not share your data with any other company. We do not sell your data.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account ID, your email address, your profile, your runs, and your leaderboard entries.

After a deletion, we keep your old username blocked for 30 days, without any link to you. This stops another player from taking the name at once and pretending to be you. After 30 days, the name is free again.

7. How to delete your account

  1. Open HEXVIBE and tap Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your progress on the device stays. If you cannot open the app, send an email to minhlong2225@gmail.com from the address of your account. We will delete the account in 30 days or less.

8. Children

Everyone can play HEXVIBE offline. Before the app shows sign-in or the leaderboards, it asks for your birth year. If you are under 16, the app turns off all online features, and we collect no personal data from you. If you think that a child under 16 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send your request to minhlong2225@gmail.com. We will answer in 30 days or less. You can also make a complaint to the data protection authority in your country.

10. Security

The app sends all data to Firebase over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Only your signed-in account can change your profile and send your scores.

11. Changes to this policy

A later version of HEXVIBE can add new features, for example ads. Before such a version is available, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top of this page.
