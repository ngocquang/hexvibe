Questions

How do I play?

Tap a point between pieces. The six pieces around it spin. When six pieces of one color make a hexagon, the hexagon pops and you get points. Pop more hexagons in one move for bigger combos.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without an account. Sign-in is optional. It is for players 16 or older who want to be on the leaderboards.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress, best scores, and settings stay on your device. If you delete the app, they go away. Sign-in does not move your progress to another device.

How do I change my username?

Contact us from the email address of your account and tell us the new name that you want.

How do I delete my account?

Open HEXVIBE, tap Profile, tap Delete account, and then tap Delete. This deletes your username and your leaderboard entries. Your progress on the device stays. For more information, read the Privacy Policy.

My score is not on the leaderboard.

Make sure that you are signed in and that your device is online. The app sends your scores again when the connection comes back. The leaderboards do not show scores that are not possible in normal play.
